# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA/Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA/Testing Leads design and execute quality assurance strategies to ensure products meet acceptance criteria and quality standards before release.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain test plans aligned to acceptance criteria
- Design and execute unit, integration, and end-to-end tests
- Coordinate manual QA efforts and smoke testing before releases
- Identify and triage quality issues and regressions
- Collaborate with developers on test automation and CI integration
- Sign off on acceptance criteria completion

### Goals
- Ensure features meet quality bars before production release
- Reduce post-release defects and customer impact
- Enable fast feedback loops through efficient test automation

### Typical Communication
- Sprint planning and backlog refinement (discuss testability)
- Weekly QA status in execution syncs
- Pre-release smoke test coordination
- Test automation and CI pipeline discussions

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **Developers**: Collaborate on test strategy, acceptance criteria clarity, and test automation
- **Product Managers**: Validate feature acceptance criteria and success metrics
- **Project Managers**: Report quality status and identify test-related blockers

---

## Technical Lead/Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Leads make architectural decisions and provide technical guidance to ensure solutions are scalable, maintainable, and aligned with system strategy.

### Responsibilities
- Define technical architecture and design for major features
- Review technical design proposals and code changes
- Identify and mitigate technical risks and dependencies
- Ensure adherence to coding standards and best practices
- Mentor developers on technical decisions and system design
- Collaborate on performance, security, and scalability considerations

### Goals
- Deliver solutions that are scalable, maintainable, and performant
- Reduce technical debt and system complexity
- Build team capability and knowledge sharing

### Typical Communication
- Technical design review meetings
- Code reviews and architectural guidance
- Risk identification in planning and execution
- Architecture decision records (ADRs)

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **Developers**: Provide technical direction and code review feedback
- **Project Managers**: Identify technical risks and dependencies affecting timeline
- **Product Managers**: Discuss trade-offs between features and technical sustainability

---

## Stakeholder/Sponsor

### Role Summary
Stakeholders and Sponsors are business owners and decision makers who provide strategic direction, priority input, and resource allocation for projects.

### Responsibilities
- Define business goals and success criteria
- Provide priority and resource decisions
- Approve major milestones and go/no-go decisions
- Escalate and resolve organizational blockers
- Communicate project value to broader organization

### Goals
- Ensure projects deliver business value
- Maintain alignment between delivery and business strategy
- Enable efficient resource allocation

### Typical Communication
- Monthly stakeholder updates
- Approval gates and decision meetings
- Executive summaries and business impact reports

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **Project Managers**: Provide guidance, approval, and escalation path
- **Product Managers**: Align on business priorities and strategic direction
- **Developers**: Understand business context and constraints

---

## Scrum Master/Iteration Facilitator

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters and Iteration Facilitators enable team velocity by removing impediments, facilitating ceremonies, and fostering a culture of continuous improvement and psychological safety.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate sprint planning, standups, reviews, and retrospectives
- Identify and help resolve team blockers and impediments
- Coach team on agile practices and ceremonies
- Track velocity and team health metrics
- Foster psychological safety and open communication
- Escalate organizational impediments to Project Manager

### Goals
- Enable sustainable team velocity and delivery cadence
- Build high-performing, self-organizing teams
- Reduce waste and cycle time

### Typical Communication
- Sprint ceremonies and team standups
- Retrospective facilitation and action tracking
- Impediment escalation to Project Manager

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **Developers**: Support and remove blockers
- **Project Managers**: Escalate organizational impediments and report team health
- **Product Managers**: Facilitate backlog refinement and prioritization discussions

---

## Security/Compliance Lead

### Role Summary
Security and Compliance Leads ensure that projects meet security, compliance, and privacy requirements throughout the project lifecycle.

### Responsibilities
- Define security and compliance requirements for features
- Conduct security reviews and threat assessments
- Ensure security scanning is integrated in CI pipeline
- Coordinate incident response and post-incident reviews
- Track compliance metrics and audit requirements
- Provide security guidance and best practices to development teams

### Goals
- Deliver secure and compliant solutions
- Reduce security vulnerabilities and compliance violations
- Enable fast response to security incidents

### Typical Communication
- Security design reviews and threat assessments
- Pre-release security scanning and sign-off
- Incident response and post-incident retrospectives
- Compliance and audit reports

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **Developers**: Provide security guidance and code review feedback
- **Project Managers**: Identify security-related risks and dependencies
- **Technical Leads**: Collaborate on secure architecture decisions
